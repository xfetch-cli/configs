<h2 id="before-opening-an-issue" align="center">Before Opening an Issue</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Confirm your xfetch version (<code>xfetch --version</code>).</li>
  <li>Identify which preset you use and how you load it (<code>--config &lt;path&gt;</code>).</li>
  <li>Include the config file and the expected vs actual output.</li>
  <li>Presets under <code>plugins/</code> require the matching plugin binaries — check <code>xfetch plugin list</code>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="response-expectations" align="center">Response Expectations</h2>

<p>
  Maintainers triage reports based on impact and reproducibility. Clear, minimal reproductions are resolved faster.
</p>
